Palette

A board for the Chapter 8 exercise. Define colors by name and RGB values (%), give each a role (Main, Secondary, Pair, One scene, Special, White, Key white), then pick the two colors that show at the same time in each section. Sections are loaded exactly as you wrote them in Listening Notes and the Song Map.

Palette

Two colors per section

Press colors in the table to pick the colors that show at the same time in that section. The first color you pick is the base color, the next is the secondary color. The key white that lights the face isn’t counted, so don’t pick it here.
