Fixture Roles

A board for filling in Table 6-4 from Chapter 6. Give each fixture row its roles and the attributes it can change, and it counts the quantity for each role and points out empty roles and attributes that don’t fit the role. If a fixture has two roles, the one listed first is its main role.

Of the attributes, dimmer, color, position and beam are the four a fixture changes itself. Time (fade and delay) is set by the console, so every fixture has it. Press a role chip to remove it; press a second chip to move it to the front (main role).
