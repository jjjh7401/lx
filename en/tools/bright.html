Brightness Plan

A board for the Chapter 10 exercise. It loads the sections and energy from the Song Map and sets, per section, the brightness of the main role and of the base (wash). The key is one value all through the song. Also write the bar where each dark cue and special cue first comes out. The percentages are console values and don’t match how bright the eye feels. Use them to set the order between sections, and check the real difference on the stage and on the monitor.
