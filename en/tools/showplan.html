Three levels of groups

Effect groups are groups of the same fixtures picked in a different order (Section 24.3). Odd/even and outside → in can also be made with MAtricks (Group 2, Wings 2) (Chapter 26).

Number ranges

A song’s number range starts at song n × 100 + 1 (Table 29-2 in Chapter 29). Change the First Index of a preset pool to the song’s starting number and the presets you store next go into that range.
