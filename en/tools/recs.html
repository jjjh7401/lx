Section Guide

The board for the Chapter 12 practice. From the sections, energy and BPM in the song structure table, it shows the starting values for each section (Tables 12-1, 12-2 and 12-4) and sets your own design from the look sheet and brightness plan beside them. Write a reason in every cell that differs from the starting values. The starting values are based on the practice stage. The default is to adjust them to your own fixtures and song.
