Flash rate Broadcast 3 Hz · venue 4 Hz

Broadcast 3 Hz is the standard for when more than 25% of the screen area flashes (Ofcom, ITU-R BT.1702). Venue 4 Hz is the upper limit recommended by the UK HSE. One flash = one pair of getting bright and getting dark.

Bar time table

Formulas: 1 beat = 60 ÷ BPM · 1 bar = beats × 1 beat · start of bar n = first downbeat + (n − 1) × 1 bar · Hz = BPM ÷ 60 × flashes per beat. If you choose 6/8, the big beat (dotted quarter) counts as the beat, and you enter the BPM in big beats too. Web tool 3 of Chapter 2 in this book.
