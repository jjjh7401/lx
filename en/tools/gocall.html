GO Practice

A worksheet for the Chapter 31 exercise. It plays a click at the song’s BPM, counts the bars and beats, and shows standby when a cue to press approaches. When you press the GO button or the space bar, it records how many seconds before or after the cue’s beat (usually the downbeat) you pressed. You can also practice to audio you load yourself; the audio plays only inside this browser and is not saved.

Press Start and it counts one bar before you go in.

You can also press with the space bar (Space).

Cues to press

Loads the cues of the Cue Sheet (Web tool 14). For this practice it keeps only the cues at section boundaries and leaves out the short cues between bars and the cues that go on by Time or Follow (the GO cue list of Table 31-5).

Results

A negative number means you pressed before the reference. The reference is the cue’s beat (usually the downbeat), the starting value of Chapter 14, and it is good if you land within 0.08 s either side (starting value). When people count the beats and tap, they usually tap about 0.02–0.1 s ahead. If the light looks late on your equipment, move it a little earlier in rehearsal (Section 31.2).
