While it plays, press a button or key at each spot where something changes. The spot snaps to the nearest beat and is written as a bar number. You can also stop, click the map to move, and then press.

Name sections right away

Listen with your eyes closed and pick one or two colors that come to mind for each section. The section that is playing lights up. Sections are split by the “Section start” marks you wrote in pass 2.

Mood starting points: bright, happy songs in yellow and orange; sad songs in blue and lavender; hot songs in red; calm songs in green and teal. These are starting points, not answers (Chapters 1 and 8).
