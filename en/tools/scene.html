Scene Viewer

See in pictures the scenes the book describes in bars, like “bars 82–83”. Pick a section on the Song Map or drag out a range, and you can play back what happens in the music and how the stage lighting moves in that stretch. A song other than the practice song works the same way, as long as you have filled in its Song Map, Palette, Look Sheet and Brightness Plan. The stage picture is based on the practice stage (Chapter 22), so it may differ from the fixture layout at your venue.

Dragging on the Song Map also changes the range.
