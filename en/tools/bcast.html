Broadcast Check

The board for the Chapter 17 practice. For each cue that flashes or changes by a cut, write the beat unit, the area it takes on screen, how long it lasts and whether red is involved. It calculates how many times per second it flashes (Hz) and compares that with the broadcast flash standard (an area larger than a quarter of the screen must not flash more than three times a second).

Flashing cues

Standards: ITU-R BT.1702, Ofcom. Check each domestic broadcaster’s own standard with that broadcaster. Estimate the area as the share of the wide-shot picture that flashes.
