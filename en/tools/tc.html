Timecode Table

A worksheet for the exercise in Chapter 28. It loads the cue sheet (Web tool 14), splits it into a track for the scene sequence and one for each rhythm layer, and converts each event’s bar and beat to a time and a timecode (hours:minutes:seconds:frames). When a layer turns on or off is read from the words in the cue sheet’s “Layers” column (on, off, held still, paused, All layers off). Enter the number of events you recorded on the console and it compares them track by track.

Leading places each event slightly ahead of the downbeat to cover the time between the console receiving a command and the light coming out. Start at 0 and adjust it in rehearsal.

Track

In Keywords, enter, separated by commas, the words in the cue sheet’s Layers column that point to this track (e.g. back low bass, floor beam). Recorded is the number of events you counted in the console’s Timecode window.
