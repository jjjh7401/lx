Phaser Calculator

A worksheet for the exercises in Chapters 25 and 26. Enter the song’s BPM and Measure and the steps’ value, Width and Transition, and it calculates the length of one cycle and the flashes per second and draws the waveform. Choose the number of fixtures and MAtricks (Block, Group, Wings) and it shows the phase each fixture receives and the last phase to enter, depending on where you enter it.

Speed and steps

Values are 0–100 (% for a dimmer; the size of the relative value for a position). Width is % of one beat, and Transition is the % of that step spent changing the value. Leave Measure at 0 and the sum of the Widths becomes one cycle.
