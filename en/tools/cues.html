Cue Sheet

The board for the Chapter 16 practice. From what you wrote in the song structure table, look sheet, brightness plan and beat grid, it builds a first draft cue sheet and points out the items among the fourteen self-checks that a tool can see. For each flagged item, choose one of keep, fix or change.

Cue Sheet

Numbering: a whole number in multiples of 10 for each section, a decimal (30.1) for inserted cues. A repeated section copies the previous round and adds one new element. The key sits outside this table. Cues a hand has trouble following, like a hit one beat after a dark cue, are set with Follow or timecode.

Fourteen self-checks

    The check does not block; it lets you know. Keep means it was intended, so it stays; fix means changing the values of one cue; change means going back to the Part 2 design and correcting it there.
