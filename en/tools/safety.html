Safety Check

A worksheet for the Chapter 32 exercise. Choose a unit for each flashing cue and it calculates how many times a second it flashes and compares that with the broadcast (three times) and live (four times) standards. It gathers on one sheet the length of cues aimed at the audience, the items to agree on haze and special effects, the weight and power of hung gear, and makes a checklist to send to the venue and the rigging crew.

Flashing cues

Flashes per second = BPM ÷ 60 ÷ Measure (per fixture). Broadcast: an area larger than a quarter of the screen must not flash more than three times a second (ITU-R BT.1702, Ofcom). Stage: strobes must not flash more than four times a second (the UK HSE’s recommendation for clubs). A phased chase bursts more often across the whole stage, so for cues that cover the audience widely, also check the count for the whole stage.

Cues aimed at the audience

Use blinders and audience positions briefly. Cues that stay for more than a bar are flagged. For a cue where a laser points toward the audience, write the company’s measurements and safety measures too (Section 32.3).
