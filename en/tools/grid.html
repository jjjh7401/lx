Beat Grid

The board for the Chapter 11 practice. Rows are roles, and cells are pieces of a section about four bars long. Press a cell to write what that role does there, and mark whether it rides the rhythm (♪) and whether it is that cell’s solo (★, the group that steps forward for four bars within the main group). Put no solo in a verse where the key is the main. When you play, the current cell is marked in time with the click.
