Lighting the Music

When the music changes, the light changes.

A free online book on lighting to music. Listen to the song, write it down in bars, decide what to show and what to hold back, then build the cues on grandMA3 and run the show. Small browser tools let you try each step with your own songs.

Jaehyun Jung · Written with AI · Free to read
